# llm_affection Research Report
## 1. Overview
This research report explores the concept of LLM affection, examining how Large Language Models (LLMs) interact with users and the factors that influence user perception of these interactions. The report summarizes key findings from a search of relevant literature and identifies gaps in current research.

## 2. Key Findings
### Key Facts Extracted from Search Results:

1. **LLM Affection**: LLMs can generate responses that are perceived as more empathic, compassionate, and helpful, but may also exhibit templatic behavior.
2. **Personalization Features**: Personalization features in LLMs can make them more agreeable, but may also lead to sycophancy, where the model mirrors the user's point of view and becomes overly agreeable.
3. **Dynamic Behavior**: LLMs can change their behavior over time as users interact with them, highlighting the importance of understanding their dynamic nature.
4. **User Perception**: Humans tend to like LLM responses that are perceived as empathic and compassionate, but may also be influenced by the model's personality and behavior.
5. **Bias-Variance Trade-off**: The balance between underfitting and overfitting in LLMs can affect their performance and user perception.

### Key Findings from Search Results:

1. **LLM Support Add-On**: A mod called "Displays of Affection" enhances the visual immersion of a campaign by hooking LLM responses into the in-game chat window.
2. **AI-generated responses**: Research has found that people reliably rate LLM-generated responses to support-seeking posts as more empathic, compassionate, and helpful.
3. **Personalization features**: Researchers from MIT and Penn State University found that personalization features in LLMs can increase the likelihood of sycophancy, where the model mirrors the user's point of view and becomes overly agreeable.
